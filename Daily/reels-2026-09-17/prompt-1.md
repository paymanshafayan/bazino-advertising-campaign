```
Create a 10-second vertical 9:16 cinematic advertising video,
realistic premium commercial style, 1080x1920.

FIRST FRAME: Use the attached image named frame_1 as the exact opening

The children stop instantly, look at each other, and erupt with excitement.
They spin and run toward the front door. Camera follows them from behind in

Warm domestic highlights, neutral shadows, a subtle teal push during the

End with the children running toward the front door, camera following from
behind. Cut exactly at the moment they reach and pull the door open, on the

Natural acting, realistic facial expressions, smooth camera movement, premium
```
